# Two-location training weeks

Load when an athlete splits the week between two places with different
equipment, terrain or time. Never assume where they are: ask before pulling
weather or prescribing outdoor sessions.

## Anchor pattern

1. **Find the fixed anchor** — a recurring, non-negotiable session or
   constraint (e.g. a weekly indoor VO2max class).
2. **Build outward from it:** day before = light/easy, day after = recovery.
3. **Assign session types by location:** the city with indoor access and short
   weekday windows takes quality sessions; the place with roads and weekend
   time takes volume and long outdoor rides.
4. **Give a weather fallback** (indoor/outdoor) for every session.
5. **Travel days are recovery days** — see `references/pitfalls-calendar.md`.

Weight-loss alongside training: `get_coaching_knowledge("nutrition")` →
`weight_management` (modest deficit only in easy blocks). No-equipment strength
sessions: load the `strength-coaching` skill (`generate_strength_workout` with
the athlete's equipment).
