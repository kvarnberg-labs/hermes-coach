# Two-Location Training Planning

When an athlete splits their week between two cities with different
equipment access, structure the training week around fixed anchors and
let geography dictate session types.

## Pattern

1. **Find the fixed anchor** — a recurring class, session, or constraint
   that is non-negotiable (e.g. "Echelon VO2max every Wednesday").
2. **Build the week outward** from the anchor:
   - Day before anchor = light/easy (prep, not fatigue)
   - Day after anchor = recovery (respect the stimulus)
3. **Assign session types by location**:
   - Location A (e.g. Stockholm): quality sessions, indoor access, shorter weekday windows
   - Location B (e.g. Ljungskile): volume, long rides, outdoor priority, weekend flexibility
4. **Always include weather fallbacks** for each session (indoor/outdoor alternativ)
5. **Travel days are recovery days** — don't fight the schedule

## Concurrent weight loss + training

Autumn/base phase is ideal for body recomposition:
- Modest deficit (-300 to -500 kcal/day) during base/maintenance phases
- High protein (1.6-2.0 g/kg) to preserve muscle and support recovery
- Z2-dominant training burns fat without driving excessive hunger
- Target 0.25-0.5 kg/week — slow and sustainable
- Avoid deficit during high-intensity blocks or race prep
- Transition to maintenance calories by winter (November)

## Bodyweight-only strength for cyclists

When the athlete has no equipment, use unilateral loading and volume:
- Pass A (legs + core): Bulgarian split squats, single-leg glute bridges,
  walking lunges, plank variations
- Pass B (upper + core + plyo): push-ups, inverted rows, dead bugs,
  jump squats
- 2×20 min/week, integrated after easy Z2 rides
- Progress via reps, not load: when hitting 3×15, add a fourth set
